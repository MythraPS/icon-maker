# Icon Maker

Make your own home-screen icons on Android. Pick an app (or a web link), pick a picture, crop it, give it a name, and the icon is added to your home screen.

- **No ads, no internet access, no permissions.** The app never goes online.
- **No badge.** Icons are added as 1×1 widgets, so they don't get the small "made by another app" badge that Android puts on shortcuts.
- **Crop your picture.** Drag to move, pinch to zoom. The circle shows exactly what the icon will look like.
- **Web links too.** Make an icon for any web page, not just apps.

Android 8.0 or newer.

## Install

Download `IconMaker.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/MythraPS/icon-maker/releases/latest) and open it on your phone. Your phone will ask you to allow installing apps from that source.

## How to use

1. **Choose app**, or **Or: use a web link** and paste an address.
2. **Choose picture**, then drag and pinch to crop it. Tap **Use**.
3. Check the name under the icon.
4. **Add to home screen**, and confirm on the screen your phone shows.

Android doesn't let any app change another app's real icon. To replace one, add your new icon, then long-press the original on your home screen and choose **Remove**. The app stays installed.

## Build

With Android Studio, or from the command line:

```
./gradlew assembleRelease
```
